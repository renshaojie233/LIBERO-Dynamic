Latency-aware dynamic manipulation

Objects keep moving while the policy thinks.

LIBERO-Dynamic extends all 40 LIBERO tasks with contact-driven moving objects, three trajectory families, three speed tiers, and calibrated model-specific inference latency. It evaluates how perception, planning, and action execution interact when the observation used by a policy can become stale before its actions reach the robot.

Pretrained policies

Execution strategies

Timing and queue continuity change dynamic-task performance even when the policy checkpoint is fixed.

Training with dynamic data

Each evaluated architecture improves under at least one dynamic-data recipe; the gain varies with temporal conditioning and data composition.

Aggregate tables are released as CSV files in results/. VLASH uses its own delay-aware checkpoint and is shown as a system reference rather than a same-checkpoint controller ablation.
